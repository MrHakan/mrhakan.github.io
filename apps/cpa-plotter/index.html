Relative-motion plot

Own ship starts at the center. Target position is shown relative to own ship; the dashed line shows target relative motion for the next 60 minutes. East is right, north is up.

Vessel data
True courses clockwise from north; speeds in knots. Bearing is true from own ship to target. Range in nautical miles.
Training only. Assumes constant course and speed on a flat local plane. Not an ARPA, COLREGs assessment, lookout substitute or navigational decision aid.
