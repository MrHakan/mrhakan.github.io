Reading the numbers

Positive corrected GM produces a positive small-angle righting arm. A free-surface correction reduces the effective GM, potentially changing its sign. At 0° the approximate arm and moment are zero even when GM is positive. The plot is limited to 10° on purpose: extrapolating this formula to large heel angles is unsafe.
