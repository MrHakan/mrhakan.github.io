Field manual

Claim more boxes than your opponent.
  • Tap an empty line between two neighboring dots.
  • The fourth side of a square claims it for you.
  • Close one or two boxes and you draw again.
  • A line that closes nothing passes the turn.
  • Highest score wins when all lines are drawn.

Play against a local, risk-aware computer or pass the device to a friend. All line targets are keyboard-focusable buttons; use Tab and Enter/Space. Undo rewinds your last turn against the computer or one move in two-player mode.
