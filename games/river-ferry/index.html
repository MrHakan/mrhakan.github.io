    How to play

    Only the ferryman can steer. Select up to the boat's passenger limit from the bank where the boat is, then press Cross. Empty return trips are allowed. Dangerous pairs are safe while the ferryman is on their bank.

    Keyboard: 1–6 toggle a passenger on the current bank, Enter cross, U undo, H hint, Esc clear selection. Hints find the shortest safe route from your current position.
