How to play

  1. Select a blue peg, then an empty hole two spaces away.
  2. Jump over exactly one peg horizontally or vertically. The jumped peg disappears.
  3. Leave exactly one peg in the gold ★ centre hole to win.
  4. All eight starting positions have a verified path to the centre. Wrong turns can strand pegs; use Undo or Hint.

Keyboard: Tab to a hole, Enter or Space to select/jump. Arrow keys navigate the board; Escape clears selection.

Daily puzzle uses the UTC date. Progress stays in this browser only. No account, ads or network requests.
