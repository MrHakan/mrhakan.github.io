    How to play

    1. Start with the word at the top.
    2. Change exactly one letter to form another English word. Do not rearrange letters.
    3. Every rung must be in the built-in word bank. No repeated rungs.
    4. Reach the destination. Any valid route wins; par is the shortest route through this word bank.

    Eight handpicked puzzles rotate daily in UTC. Progress and records stay on this device. Hints find a shortest route from your current rung, even after a detour.

    Tip: Use Possible moves to see every accepted one-letter change. Tap one to fill the input. Enter adds a word; Ctrl+Z undoes a move.
