Chase rules

0 / 10 sideways moves

D = hound · R = hare. Two local players alternate. Hounds move right or sideways along a line; the hare moves in any direction. Only empty points are legal, and no pieces are captured.

Hounds win by trapping the hare. The hare wins by getting strictly left of every hound, or after ten consecutive sideways hound moves.

Choose a piece, then a highlighted point. Use Tab and Enter or Space with a keyboard.
