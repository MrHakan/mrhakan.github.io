How to play

🧑‍🔧 Walk into a crate to push it one square. A wall or another crate blocks the push. Place all crates on the glowing targets.

Keyboard: arrows / WASD to move, U or Ctrl+Z to undo, R to restart, H for a hint. Swipe the board or use the direction pad on mobile.

Watch the corners! A crate stuck outside a target may require Undo. Your best pushes and moves are saved only on this device. Hints do not qualify for records.

Daily warehouse rotates at 00:00 UTC. All eight levels are always playable.
