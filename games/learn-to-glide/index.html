how to play

a penguin, a ramp on a cliff, and an ice wall 3200 m out on the sea ice. smash it at 50 m/s or faster before the season's 30 days run out.

  • every launch is one day. distance, height, top speed and popped balloons pay cash.
  • spend it in the hangar: ramp, sled, glider, rockets and payload (more cash per metre).
  • in the air: ↑/W nose up, ↓/S nose down, hold space for rockets. on a phone use the buttons under the sky.
  • nose up too far and the wing stalls. a shallow glide goes far; a dive at the end goes fast.
  • balloons give a kick, clouds are soggy and slow you down.
  • enter launches and continues, p pauses. fewer days = better medal.
