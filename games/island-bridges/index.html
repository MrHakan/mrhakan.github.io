How to play

Hashiwokakero rules
  • Each island's number is the total bridges touching it.
  • Connect two islands with 0, 1 or 2 straight bridges.
  • Bridges cannot cross or pass through an island.
  • All islands must belong to one connected network.
Click a pale line repeatedly to cycle 0 → 1 → 2 → 0. Keyboard: Tab to a line, Enter/Space to change it. U = undo, H = hint.
