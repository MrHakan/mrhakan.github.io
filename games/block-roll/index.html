block roll 98 - how to play

stand it up in the hole.

you are a 1 x 1 x 2 block on floating tiles. roll it around and drop it upright into the dark hole. roll off the edge and the level starts over.

  • arrows / WASD, swipe, click beside the block or use the pad to roll
  • orange floor cracks if you stand on it upright
  • round switch: any touch. x switch: stand on it. both work bridges
  • splitter: you become two cubes, space swaps, bump them side by side to merge
  • R restarts, P pauses. every level has a six digit passcode
