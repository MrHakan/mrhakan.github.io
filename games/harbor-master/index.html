how to keep port.exe running

Mouse / touch: select a ship or its manifest button, then tap the chart to add route points. Tap its labelled berth for the final approach. Routes happen immediately; watch the other traffic.

Keyboard: select a manifest button, focus the chart, use ← ↑ ↓ → to move the crosshair and Enter to add a waypoint. Backspace removes the last point; Escape clears the route. The matching-berth button works without the chart.

Each safe docking earns 100. A collision, wrong berth or grounding ends the watch. New ships wait outside if the entrance is busy. Hidden tabs pause; resume when you are ready. This is arcade fiction, not seamanship training.
