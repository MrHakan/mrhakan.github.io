How to play

Classic English 33-hole cross board. Tap or keyboard-activate a peg, then choose a highlighted landing hole. A legal jump moves two spaces horizontally or vertically.

Undo as often as you like. Hints highlight one legal move, but do not guarantee a winning route.

Works entirely in your browser, without accounts or tracking.
