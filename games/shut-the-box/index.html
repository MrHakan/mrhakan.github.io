How to play

Roll two dice. Choose any combination of open numbers whose sum equals the roll, then close them. Repeat until no legal combination remains or all tiles are closed.

The sum of remaining tiles is your score. Zero is perfect. This version always uses two dice. Use Tab and Enter or Space to operate the buttons.
