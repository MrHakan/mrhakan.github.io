Field manual

Take the final token to win.
  1. Players alternate turns.
  2. Choose one pile and remove one or more tokens from that pile.
  3. You cannot take from two piles in the same turn.
  4. The player who takes the very last token wins.

Each coin button removes that coin and all coins to its right. For example, the third coin in a five-coin pile takes three coins. All boards are winnable when you find the right move.
