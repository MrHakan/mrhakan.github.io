📖 How to play

Move the whole tower from A to C, one top disk at a time. Never place a larger disk on a smaller disk. The theoretical minimum is 2ⁿ − 1 moves.

Keyboard: 1, 2, 3 choose pegs; Escape cancels selection; U undoes; H suggests a shortest-path move. Hints work even after mistakes.
