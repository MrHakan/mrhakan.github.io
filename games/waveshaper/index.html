Ready. Four buttons are all you need.Keyboard / touch

W / S — square up / down. ↑ / ↓ — triangle up / down. Tap the four panels on a phone. Press once per bend; holding a button will not repeat.

Match in order. You can clear the closest visible bend as soon as you read it. Speed rises with your score. In Normal, every 20 consecutive matches restores one life.

Gamepad: LB / LT = square up / down. RB / RT = triangle up / down. Or use Y / A and X / B (Xbox labels). Start / Menu starts or pauses. Connect a standard controller and press a button to detect it.

An independent browser remake inspired by WAVESHAPER by GAMES FOR HUMANS. Original code, vector art and synthesized audio for this site. Records stay on this device; Practice has no record.

Comfort & shortcuts

P / Esc pauses, R starts a fresh run, M mutes, F toggles fullscreen. Leaving the tab pauses automatically. Reduced motion removes particles, trails and pulses; the approaching wave remains visible to play. Best scores are separate for Normal and Hard.
